About Smagnet

Engineering for systems that have to work

Smagnet is a software and data engineering consultancy based in Miami Beach, Florida. We design, build and run data-intensive systems for companies where reliability, latency and data quality have a direct impact on revenue.

Our experience

Our team brings more than 12 years of hands-on experience as architects, data engineers and quantitative developers, delivering production systems for investment banks, payment processors, asset managers, crypto exchanges, e-commerce brands and public institutions across the US and Europe.

Representative work:

  • Payment platforms and event-driven Kafka architectures for a global bank
  • Pricing libraries and data platforms for a European banking group
  • Real-time ETF valuation (iNAV) for an electronic trading venue
  • Machine-learning risk models for a global investment bank
  • A 120 TB data platform migration to Google Cloud, with demand forecasting, for an automotive manufacturer
  • Azure data architecture for an EU agency
  • Low-latency market making connected to 30+ crypto exchanges
  • Amazon advertising and marketplace automation for brands selling in 11 countries
