Sustainability

Our commitment to the SDGs

We align our work with the Sustainable Development Goals of the United Nations 2030 Agenda, applied to what we actually do: building software, data and cloud systems.

SDG 9

Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

We build reliable, well-documented digital infrastructure (data platforms, APIs and cloud systems) that our clients can run and evolve for years, not throwaway prototypes.

SDG 8

Decent Work and Economic Growth

We automate repetitive work (reporting, stock control, campaign management) so teams spend their time on decisions, and we help small sellers compete in global marketplaces.

SDG 12

Responsible Consumption and Production

We extend and modernize existing systems before rewriting them, and we size infrastructure for real demand instead of buying capacity that sits idle.

SDG 13

Climate Action

Efficient code and right-sized cloud resources use less energy. We review idle servers, oversized clusters and unnecessary data processing in every project, which lowers both the bill and the carbon footprint.

Smagnet LLC is not endorsed or certified by the United Nations. This page explains how we align our work with its goals.

Carbon footprint

Software has a physical cost: every server, query and page view uses electricity. In client projects we treat efficiency as a requirement:

  • Right-sizing and auto-scaling cloud resources, and switching off idle environments.
  • Processing only the data that is needed, incrementally instead of full reloads.
  • Choosing efficient languages and architectures (for example Rust for hot paths) where it matters.
  • Choosing cloud regions and providers that report their energy sources.

This website
